What it does

01 / Map

Draw your fixtures, or import them

Lay out the installation as it is built: every pixel where it really sits.

  • Lines, matrices, circles and ovals, curved paths and a pen
  • Import an SVG drawing of the building as fixtures or as a guide
  • Group, lock, align, rotate, resize, copy and paste
  • Pixel count, colour order and wiring direction per fixture

02 / Play

Effects, shaders, video and capture

From slow washes for architecture to disco, on any part of the layout.

  • 32 built-in generators, sorted by mood, use, look and motion
  • Your own ISF shaders, images and video: trim, loop, with or without sound
  • Window capture, for example Resolume's output
  • Zones and layers with blend modes; colour wheel, RGB and HSV

03 / Scenes

Store a look. Fade back to it

Build the show once, then play it by hand or let it run by itself.

  • Scenes per zone with crossfades; the fade time sits on each scene
  • Playlists of scenes with play times: in a loop, once, or handing over to another
  • Keys 1–9 play the scenes of the selected zone

04 / Patch

Patch once. Send Art‑Net

Drag fixtures onto controller outputs and the addresses follow.

  • Automatic addressing, node discovery and a DMX monitor
  • 16-bit fixtures, dimming curves, RGBW, gamma and colour trims
  • Highlight lights fixtures on site: Trace, Flood, Color check, Spotlight, Pixel finder
  • A CSV patch list for the install crew

05 / MIDI

Learn your controller in a second

Press Learn, move a fader, click what it should move.

  • Master, blackout, group dimmers, zone levels, scenes and playlists
  • Macros: speed, intensity, saturation and colours for every effect at once
  • Any control of an effect; faders pick up where the slider is
  • Every mapping as a wire in the patch bay, and a virtual controller on screen

06 / Remote

Hand a phone to the client

Phones, tablets and other computers on the network log in with a role.

  • Programmer: everything, with a password
  • Operator: content, scenes and playlists; the mapping stays locked
  • Remote: a simple page to play scenes and playlists and dim each zone

07 / Look

Black for the show. White for daylight

The interface is black, so the light on the stage is the brightest thing on screen. One click turns it white for working outside.

  • Works offline: fonts and everything else come with the app
  • The output keeps running when the window is minimised or covered
  • Projects save to a file you can carry to the site
